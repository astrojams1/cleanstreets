# Reply templates

Skeletons, not scripts. Swap the bracketed parts, cut anything that does not
apply, and keep the whole body under five sentences plus signature. Signature
and ref-code rules: `.claude/skills/README.md`.

Patreon links always carry a ref code: `https://www.patreon.com/cleanstreets?ref=inbox-<category>`.

## intake-form, work request

Subject: reply in thread

> Hi [First name], thanks for reaching out about [block or area]. Next step is
> simple: tell me the cross streets and roughly how often you'd want the block
> covered, and I'll come back with what's realistic. Clean Streets is funded by
> neighbors chipping in monthly, so coverage follows where the support is;
> here's how that works if you're curious: https://www.patreon.com/cleanstreets?ref=inbox-intake-form
>
> [signature]

## intake-form, wants to stay informed or support

> Hi [First name], glad you found us. Easiest way to stay in the loop and back
> the crew is Patreon, where the monthly update goes out:
> https://www.patreon.com/cleanstreets?ref=inbox-intake-form. If you've got a
> corner in the Mission that always looks rough, tell me which one; it helps
> us aim.
>
> [signature]

## service-request (direct email, not via form)

> Hi [First name], thanks for the note about [what they described]. Two
> questions so I can give you a straight answer: which cross streets, and are
> you thinking daily, weekly, or a one-time pass? Quick background: we're a
> neighbor-funded crew of paid cleaners, not a contractor, so I'll be honest
> about what we can cover.
>
> [signature]

Do not include pricing, even if asked. If they asked, add: "On cost, James
will follow up directly once he knows the block."

## reply to a question about a skill-sent email (ref code in quoted text)

Answer the question in one or two sentences, in the same thread. If the
answer requires anything from the "no commitments" list, draft instead.

## meeting or call requested (any category where a reply is allowed)

Add this line instead of agreeing to a time:

> Email's actually fastest for me right now; if you tell me [the thing the
> call was for], I can get you an answer today.

## job-application (only when applications are open)

> Hi [First name], thanks for thinking of Clean Streets. We're not hiring at
> the moment, but I'll keep your note. If that changes I'll reach out.
>
> [signature]
