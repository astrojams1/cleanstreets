# Post style for field updates

Posts are from James, in the voice of someone who walks these blocks and
pays the crew. Short, specific, visual. The photo does the persuading.

## Shape

- **Title**: the block or the week. "Stevenson and McCoppin, Tuesday" or
  "This week on 24th Street". Never "Update" alone.
- **Body**: three to five sentences.
  1. Where the crew was and what they cleared, in the words a neighbor
     would use (bags, needles never mentioned by count, sweep, curb line,
     the corner by the taqueria). Match the photos.
  2. One concrete detail from a photo: what it looked like before, what it
     looks like after.
  3. Optional: the running total from `data/impact.csv` if it moved this
     month ("that puts the crew past 1,400 paid hours").
  4. The link, in one sentence: "Neighbors fund every hour of this;
     if you'd like to keep it going on your block:
     https://www.patreon.com/cleanstreets?ref=post-<yyyymmdd>".
- **Photos**: two to four. A before-and-after pair beats four afters. Skip
  any photo with a readable face, a license plate, or a house number.

## Voice

- First person, present tense where it fits, no exclamation points.
- No "amazing", "incredible", "so grateful". Say what happened.
- Name streets and cross streets, never people.
- One post per run; if two blocks were cleaned, pick the one with the
  better photos and mention the other in a clause.

## Audience

Growth posts are `public` so prospects who land on the page see them.
A patron-only post is right only for money talk (revenue, what next
month's budget covers); those come from patreon-growth's monthly post.

## Examples

> The crew took the east side of Capp between 24th and 25th on Tuesday

> yours: https://www.patreon.com/cleanstreets?ref=post-20260912
