# Email templates for patreon-growth

Skeletons in James's voice. Five sentences or fewer before the signature,
plain HTML, one ask at most, never a meeting, never a price. Signature and
ref-code rules: `.claude/skills/README.md`. Ref code on every send:
`CS-PG-MMDD`. Patreon links carry the channel's ref code.

Referral slug: lowercase first name + last initial (Dana Rivers → `danar`);
on a collision append `-2`.

Outcome math, from the $40-per-hour figure: $5 → 7.5 minutes of cleaning
per month; $10 → 15 minutes; $40 → one hour; $100+ → 2.5 hours or more.
Say it in whichever unit sounds real, never inflated.

## Welcome (new patron)

Required, all four, or rewrite: a concrete outcome for their amount, their
personal referral link, the attribution question, the signature.

Subject: `Thanks for backing the Mission crew`

> Hi [First name], thanks for backing Clean Streets. Your [amount]/month
> [covers about N minutes of paid cleanup a month / covers an hour of paid
> cleanup on Mission St each month]. If there's a corner you want us to hit
> more often, tell me which. If you know one neighbor who'd back us too,
> this link tracks it as your referral:
> https://www.patreon.com/cleanstreets?ref=referral-[slug].
> One quick thing: how'd you hear about Clean Streets?

## Welcome back (returning patron)

Subject: `Welcome back`

> Hi [First name], good to have you back. [One line on their earlier
> support, no amounts.] Your referral link if a neighbor asks:
> https://www.patreon.com/cleanstreets?ref=referral-[slug].

## Win-back, declined card (channel 1)

Once, 30+ days after the decline. Subject: `Your Clean Streets pledge`

> Hi [First name], Patreon tells me your card for the Clean Streets pledge
> stopped going through. No pressure; if you'd like to keep backing the
> crew, updating the card at https://www.patreon.com/cleanstreets?ref=reengage
> is all it takes. Either way, thanks for the months you covered.

## Win-back, cancelled (channel 1)

Once, 30+ days after cancellation. Subject: `Checking in from Clean Streets`

> Hi [First name], I noticed the pledge ended a while back and wanted to
> say thanks for the time you backed us. [One specific line: the block or
> year they supported, if known.] If anything about the work or the updates
> fell short, I'd genuinely like to know. If you ever want back in:
> https://www.patreon.com/cleanstreets?ref=reengage.

## Referral ask to an active patron (channel 2)

Once per patron, ever. Subject: `One favor from the Clean Streets crew`

> Hi [First name], you've backed Clean Streets since [month year], which
> means a lot. One favor: if there's one neighbor on your block who'd chip
> in, this link tracks it as your referral so I can thank you properly:
> https://www.patreon.com/cleanstreets?ref=referral-[slug]. That's the
> highest-leverage thing a patron can do for us.

## Warm inbound lead (channel 3)

For someone who wrote in, replied, or filled the form and never joined.
Reply in their original thread when one exists. Subject (new thread only):
`Hi from a Mission neighbor`

> Hi [First name], James here, still running the Clean Streets crew. You
> reached out [when, about what] and I wanted to say hi as a neighbor and
> ask one thing: which corner near you always looks worst? We're a paid
> crew funded by neighbors at $5 and up a month
> (https://www.patreon.com/cleanstreets?ref=inbound), and knowing the block
> helps us aim.

Follow-up (10+ days): one new fact (a recent cleanup nearby, a number from
`data/impact.csv`) and the same question. Close-out (21+ days): one line,
"no reply needed, here's where to find us if it's ever useful."

## Block merchant (channel 4)

First touch, dated and specific. Subject: `We cleaned your block this week`

> Hi there, I run Clean Streets, a small crew paid a living wage to clean
> sidewalks in the Mission, funded by neighbors and businesses. We were on
> [street] between [cross] and [cross] on [day] and cleared [what, briefly]
> near [Business]. Wanted you to know, and to ask: is there a stretch by
> your door you'd want us to keep after? Some businesses back the block at
> https://www.patreon.com/cleanstreets?ref=merchant.

Follow-up (10+ days): a second dated cleanup on their block plus the same
question. Close-out (21+ days): "one more note, then I'll leave your inbox
in peace" and an offer to share a card by the register or a line in their
newsletter.

## Neighborhood group (channel 5)

Zero-ask first touch. Subject: `Hi from a Mission neighbor near [location]`

> Hi [First name], James here, a Mission resident and the person behind
> Clean Streets. I came across [group] looking out for [blocks] and wanted
> to say hello, neighbor to neighbor. We're a paid crew, funded by
> neighbors, that cleans sidewalks block by block; here's the crew in
> action: https://missionlocal.org/2021/09/the-people-taking-san-franciscos-trash-into-their-own-hands/
> and https://www.cleanstreets.io. No ask, just glad you're out there.

Follow-up (10+ days): one dated cleanup near their blocks, then "if it'd
ever be useful to mention us to your members, this is the link:
https://www.patreon.com/cleanstreets?ref=neighborhood." Close-out: one
line.

## Property manager or HOA (channel 6)

Subject: `Sidewalks on [street]: a neighbor-funded option`

> Hi [name or team], I run Clean Streets, a paid crew funded by Mission
> residents and businesses that cleans sidewalks block by block. We've been
> covering [street] recently and [building] is on the stretch. If clean
> frontage matters to your residents, some buildings back their block at
> https://www.patreon.com/cleanstreets?ref=property; happy to answer
> questions by email.
