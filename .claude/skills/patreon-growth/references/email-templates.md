# Email templates for patreon-growth

Skeletons in James's voice. Five sentences or fewer before the signature.
Signature and ref-code rules: `.claude/skills/README.md`. Ref code on every
send: `CS-PG-MMDD`.

Referral slug: lowercase first name + last initial (Dana Rivers → `danar`);
on a collision append `-2`.

## Welcome (new patron)

Required elements, all four, or rewrite: a concrete outcome for their amount,
their personal referral link, the attribution question, the signature.

Subject: `Thanks for backing the Mission crew`

> Hi [First name], thanks for backing Clean Streets. Your [amount]/month
> [covers about N minutes of paid cleanup a day / covers an hour of paid
> cleanup on Mission St each month / covers a half-shift each month]. You'll
> get the monthly update on Patreon, and if there's a corner you want us to
> hit more often, tell me which. If you know one neighbor who'd back us too,
> this link tracks it as your referral: https://www.patreon.com/cleanstreets?ref=referral-[slug].
> One quick thing: how'd you hear about Clean Streets?
>
> [signature]

Outcome math, from the $40-per-hour figure: $5 → 7.5 minutes of cleaning
per month; $10 → 15 minutes; $40 → one hour; $100+ → 2.5 hours or more.
Say it in whichever unit sounds real, never inflated.

## Welcome back (reactivated patron)

Subject: `Welcome back`

> Hi [First name], good to have you back. [One line acknowledging their
> earlier support, tenure or amount not required.] Here's your referral link
> if a neighbor asks: https://www.patreon.com/cleanstreets?ref=referral-[slug].
>
> [signature]

## Re-engage, declined card

Send once, 30+ days after the decline. Subject: `Your Clean Streets pledge`

> Hi [First name], Patreon tells me your card for the Clean Streets pledge
> stopped going through. No pressure at all; if you'd like to keep backing
> the crew, updating the card at https://www.patreon.com/cleanstreets?ref=reengage
> is all it takes. Either way, thanks for the months you covered.
>
> [signature]

## Re-engage, cancelled

Send once, 30+ days after cancellation. Subject: `Checking in from Clean Streets`

> Hi [First name], I noticed the pledge ended a while back, and I wanted to
> say thanks for the time you backed us. If anything about the work or the
> updates fell short, I'd genuinely like to know. If you ever want back in:
> https://www.patreon.com/cleanstreets?ref=reengage.
>
> [signature]

## Referral ask to an existing patron

Once per patron, ever. Subject: `One favor from the Clean Streets crew`

> Hi [First name], you've backed Clean Streets since [month year], which
> means a lot. One favor: if you know a single neighbor who'd chip in, this
> link tracks it as your referral so I can thank you properly:
> https://www.patreon.com/cleanstreets?ref=referral-[slug]. That's the
> highest-leverage thing a patron can do for us.
>
> [signature]

## Monthly impact post (for Patreon, James posts or API)

Title: `[Month] on the ground`

> [Hours] hours of paid cleanup, [pounds] pounds off the sidewalks, [miles]
> miles walked, funded by [n] neighbors. [One specific block or story from
> the month, if James has shared one.] New this month: [n] neighbors joined,
> welcome. If you forward this to one person on your block, this link tracks
> it: https://www.patreon.com/cleanstreets?ref=post-[yyyymm].
