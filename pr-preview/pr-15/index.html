Our Story

I founded Clean Streets in 2020, right at the onset of the pandemic. The streets were a mess back then—trash literally piled at intersections 24/7—and I just wanted to help.

At first, I assumed this was the city’s job. But I soon learned the policy: the city cleans the roadways (street sweepers), but residents and businesses are responsible for cleaning the sidewalks.

I didn’t love that policy then, and I don’t love it now. But until it changes, community action is the only way to keep our neighborhoods livable.

The Model

After trying everything from buying extra trash cans to cleaning corners myself, I landed on a simple model: neighbors chipping in small monthly amounts to pay crews to pick up litter.

It worked. For over 5 years, we’ve had crews running routes in the Mission and beyond. We’ve been featured by Mission Local, rallied with neighborhood action groups, and seen real, visible improvements on the blocks we cover.

The Mission

We aren't a big agency. We are a community of neighbors who believe that if we all pitch in a few dollars, we don't have to live with trash on our doorsteps.

Whether you can contribute $5 or $50, your support goes directly to paying people to do the work that keeps our sidewalks clean.

FAQ

About Clean Streets

  • Why did you start Clean Streets?

    I am a long-time San Francisco resident who has always been bothered by the litter on our streets. During the pandemic in 2020, after the city temporarily halted cleaning services, the problem got far worse. At the same time, lots of people were losing their jobs in the service industry. I envisioned Clean Streets as an opportunity to have a positive impact on the community I love, by helping address our long-standing litter problem and providing high-paying jobs.

  • Are you a non-profit organization?

    No. While we operate on a model where 100% of contributions go toward litter pickup and we do not take a profit ourselves, we have not gone through the formal legal process to establish 501(c)(3) status.

  • How are donated contributions used?

    If you make a donation, 100% of your contribution funds litter pickup. We do not take a profit. I work with the community to identify hard-hit areas and channel funds directly to cleaning those zones.

  • Are my contributions tax-deductible?

    Since Clean Streets is not a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization, contributions are likely not deductible as charitable donations for individuals. However, if you are a business owner, these contributions may be deductible as a business expense (such as maintenance or advertising). We recommend consulting a tax professional regarding your specific situation.

Service & Operations

  • Doesn't the city pick up litter?

    While the city sweeps streets, empties city trashcans, and removes large items (like mattresses) reported to SF311, businesses and residents are responsible for maintaining sidewalks.

  • If I contribute, will my specific block be cleaned?

    Not necessarily. Contributions are pooled into a general fund used to clean the areas of the city that need it most. Rather than restricting funds to a single block, we direct resources to "hard hit" zones where we can make the biggest immediate difference.

  • Is there any way to guarantee service for my specific block?

    Yes, but this requires a Sponsorship level contribution. Because activating a dedicated route for a specific location requires significantly more logistical planning and overhead, we can only guarantee service for a specific block if the contribution covers the full cost of maintaining that route. Please contact us for a quote.

  • How much does service cost?

    For general support, you can contribute any amount you wish. However, if you are looking to Sponsor a specific block to guarantee weekly service, the cost is higher than a standard donation to cover the full logistics of a dedicated route.

  • How do you decide where to clean each week?

    We operate on a flexible model. We assess the city's needs on a weekly basis and work with the community to identify high-litter areas. This allows us to send crews where they are needed most, rather than sticking to a rigid schedule that might miss a sudden accumulation of debris elsewhere.

  • Can I cancel my recurring contribution?

    Yes, you can cancel your recurring contribution at any time.

Scope of Work

  • Do you remove encampments?

    No, we do not remove encampments or disturb the homeless. We strictly enforce this policy without exception.

  • Do you remove large items?

    We don't remove large items, but we do report large items we encounter to SF311 via Twitter. SF311 typically addresses the issue within a couple of hours.

  • Do you clean private alcoves, doorways, or driveways?

    Our service is strictly for the public right-of-way (sidewalks and curbs). We do not enter private property lines, inclusive of recessed doorways, gated alcoves, or driveways, to ensure the safety of our workers and avoid liability issues.

Our Workers

  • How are workers paid?

    Workers are independent contractors who get paid 1 month in advance via PayPal at a rate approximately 2 times the minimum wage. Despite this generous pay, because we take no profit, our services cost way less than other options.
