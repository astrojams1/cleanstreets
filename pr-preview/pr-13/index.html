Our Story

I founded Clean Streets in 2020, right at the onset of the pandemic. The streets were a mess back then—trash literally piled at intersections 24/7—and I just wanted to help.

At first, I assumed this was the city’s job. But I soon learned the policy: the city cleans the roadways (street sweepers), but residents and businesses are responsible for cleaning the sidewalks.

I didn’t love that policy then, and I don’t love it now. But until it changes, community action is the only way to keep our neighborhoods livable.

The Model

After trying everything from buying extra trash cans to cleaning corners myself, I landed on a simple model: neighbors chipping in small monthly amounts to pay crews to pick up litter.

The Results

It worked. For over 5 years, we’ve had crews running routes in the Mission and beyond. We’ve been featured by Mission Local, rallied with neighborhood action groups, and seen real, visible improvements on the blocks we cover.

The Mission

We aren't a big agency. We are a community of neighbors who believe that if we all pitch in a few dollars, we don't have to live with trash on our doorsteps.

Whether you can contribute $5 or $50, your support goes directly to paying people to do the work that keeps our sidewalks clean.

FAQ

Doesn't the city pick up litter?

While the city sweeps streets, empties city trashcans, and removes large items (like mattresses) reported to SF311, businesses and residents are responsible for maintaining sidewalks.

How much does service cost?

It depends on the specific services requested, but typical weekly litter removal on a typical block in San Francisco costs $75 per month per side of the street.

How are donated contributions used?

If you make a donation, 100% of your contribution funds litter pickup. I work with the community to identify hard hit areas and channel any unused funds to cleaning those areas.

How are workers paid?

Workers are independent contractors who get paid 1 month in advance via Paypal at a rate approximately 2 times the minimum wage. We take no profit.

Do you remove encampments?

No, we do not remove encampments or disturb the homeless. We strictly enforce this policy without exception.

Why did you start Clean Streets?

I am a long time San Francisco resident who has always been bothered by the litter on our streets. I envisioned Clean Streets as an opportunity to have a positive impact on the community I love.
